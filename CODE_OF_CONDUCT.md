# Code of Conduct

This project is for everyone. Here is how we treat each other.

## Be kind

- Be friendly and patient.
- Remember that people have different skill levels. Everyone was new once.
- Ask questions. Nobody here knows everything.
- Assume people mean well. Most problems are misunderstandings.

## Do not

- Insult people, or call them names.
- Make jokes about someone's race, sex, religion, country, age, body, or
  disability.
- Post sexual images or comments.
- Bully, follow, or threaten anyone.
- Share someone's private details, like their address or phone number.
- Keep pushing after someone asks you to stop.

## When you disagree

You will disagree with people. That is fine and useful.

Argue about the code, not the person. Say what you think is wrong and why. Do
not say the other person is stupid.

If a discussion turns into an argument, stop and step away for a bit.

## Where this applies

Everywhere this project lives. Issues, pull requests, commit messages,
discussions. It also applies when you speak for the project somewhere else.

## If something goes wrong

Email **ayoubzulfiqar3@gmail.com**.

Say what happened, where, and when. Add links if you have them.

Your report stays private. We will not share your name with the person you
reported.

We will read every report and reply. We will tell you what we decided.

## What happens then

Depending on what happened, we may:

1. Ask the person to stop, in private.
2. Give a public warning.
3. Block them for a while.
4. Block them for good.

We pick based on how bad it was and whether it keeps happening.

## Thanks

This is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
rewritten in plainer words.
